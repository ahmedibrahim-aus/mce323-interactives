The modules

Each one opens on a worked example straight from your notes, so you can check it against the solution you already have. Most have a Predict mode that hides the answers — use it. Guess first, then reveal. Being wrong on purpose is how this material sticks.

How to get the most out of these

Before the problem set, not after

Open the module for whatever you are stuck on and move one slider at a time. Ask yourself which way the number should go before you drag it — if it moves the other way, you have found a gap worth chasing, and that is worth more than a correct answer you did not predict.

Arrow keys nudge the main control and Space runs the animation. Nothing to install, nothing to log into, and it works on your phone. These are not a substitute for the textbook or the lectures; they are for building the intuition that makes both easier to read.
