What to notice

  1. The hexagon is inscribed inside the ellipse. So MSS never predicts a higher factor of safety than DE — it fails first, everywhere. That single geometric fact is the whole "MSS is conservative" argument.
  2. They agree on the axes and disagree most in the fourth quadrant. Drag the point to σA positive and σB negative and watch the two numbers separate. That is exactly the state a shaft in bending plus torsion lives in.
  3. Pure hydrostatic loading never yields. Put the point on the 45° line and push it out — the ellipse follows it forever. Metals yield by distortion, not by uniform squeezing.
  4. Switch to brittle and the envelope goes lopsided, because Suc is several times Sut. Cast iron is much stronger in compression, and Coulomb–Mohr is the theory that admits it.
