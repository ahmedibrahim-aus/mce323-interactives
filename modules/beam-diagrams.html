What to notice

  1. Every jump in V is a point load; every slope in V is a distributed load. Turn w on and watch the flat shear become a ramp. Nothing else can change the shear diagram.
  2. M is the area under V, accumulated left to right. Where V crosses zero, M is at a maximum — because that is where the accumulation stops growing and starts shrinking. That is the whole reason we draw V first.
  3. Integration raises the order. A step in V integrates to a ramp in M; a ramp in V integrates to a parabola. If your M diagram has the wrong shape, check the order.
  4. The critical section is not always mid-span. Drag P₁ around and watch Mmax move. This is the whole point of drawing the diagrams instead of guessing.
