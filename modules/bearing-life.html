What to notice

  1. Double the load and a ball bearing loses seven-eighths of its life. Life goes as (C/F)a with a = 3. Shave 20% off the load instead and life almost doubles — which is usually the cheapest engineering available.
  2. Rollers are less forgiving of load and better at carrying it. Switch the type: a = 10/3 makes the line steeper still.
  3. Catalogue ratings are 90% reliability, not 100%. Ask for 99% and the required capacity jumps by roughly two thirds for the same duty. That is the Weibull correction, and it is not a small effect.
  4. Reliabilities multiply. Six bearings at 90% each give a machine at 0.9⁶ = 53%. Specifying every bearing individually at 90% is not the same as designing a reliable machine.
