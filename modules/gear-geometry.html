What to notice

  1. Two gears mesh if they share a module and a pressure angle. Nothing else has to match — not the diameter, not the tooth count. Change N and watch the pitch circles resize while the teeth still fit.
  2. The pressure line is straight, and contact runs along it. That straightness is conjugate action: the velocity ratio stays constant through the whole engagement, which is why the involute won and other profiles did not.
  3. Drop the pinion below about 18 teeth at φ = 20° and the flank gets undercut at the root — material removed where the bending stress is highest. The warning appears automatically. Raise φ to 25° and the limit falls to 12.
  4. The base circle is the generator, not a construction line. Below it there is no involute at all, only a radial flank. Watch it cross the dedendum circle as the tooth count drops — that is the moment undercutting begins.
